## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1. Data you give us

- **Phone number** - to create your account, sign in with an SMS code and protect your account;
- **Google account (name, email)** - only if you choose to sign in with Google;
- **First and last name, university** - to show you to other students and connect you with classmates;
- **University email** (optional) - to contact you about your account;
- **Profile photo** - if you upload one;
- **Your content** - listings, stories, posts, group and chat messages, photos, videos and voice messages;
- **Reports and requests** - so we can review them and reply.

### 2.2. Location

The App asks for your location only when you start a related action and only after you grant permission, for example to show the distance to a listing or discount. It is **optional**: the rest of the App works without it. We do not track your location in the background. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

### 2.3. Camera, microphone and photos

Camera, microphone and photo access is requested only when you start sending a photo, recording a voice message or a similar action. The App uses only the files you select or record.

### 2.4. Payment data

When paid features are introduced, payments will be processed by the licensed payment institution **Atmos**. **We never store your full card number or CVV.** We keep only the card token issued by Atmos, the last 4 digits of the card number, the expiry date, the card type, and the payment amount, date and status.

### 2.5. Technical data

To run and secure the service: user and device identifiers, push notification token, basic device and app version information, request time, IP address and technical error records.

## 3. Why we use your data

- creating your account and signing you in;
- connecting you with classmates, showing your profile and content;
- delivering messages and notifications;
- showing the distance to listings and discounts (only with your permission);
- accepting payment for paid features;
- reviewing reports, preventing fraud and abuse;
- legally required reporting and requests from authorities.

We **do not sell** your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. What other people can see

- Your name, profile photo, university, listings, posts and stories are visible to other students in the App.
- You choose in settings who can see your phone number and last-seen time.
- You can also limit in settings who can message you.
- Private messages are visible only to the people in that chat, and group messages only to group members.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share data only as far as needed to provide the service:

- **Eskiz** (Uzbekistan) - to deliver SMS codes: phone number and SMS text;
- **Google** - to deliver push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and if you choose to sign in with Google;
- **Atmos** (Uzbekistan) - to accept payments for paid features: card details and payment amount;
- **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Germany) - the hosting provider that runs our servers;
- **Competent authorities** - only when the law requires it, in the legally prescribed manner.

## 6. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Hetzner Online GmbH servers in Nuremberg, Germany. By using the App, you agree that your data is stored and processed outside Uzbekistan in this way.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Stories** - leave the feed after 24 hours and stay in your private archive; their files are deleted after 365 days;
- **Account data and other content** - until you delete them or your account;
- **When you delete your account** - your personal data is deleted from our servers;
- **Payment and accounting records** - as long as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

- **Access** - ask what data we hold about you;
- **Correction** - edit your profile in the App or contact us;
- **Deletion** - delete listings, posts, stories or your whole account;
- **Withdraw consent** - for example, turn off location or camera access;
- **Complaint** - contact the competent state authority.

We reply to requests within 10 business days.

### Deleting your account

In the App: you can delete your account at any time in **Settings**. When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted from our servers. If you cannot sign in, email quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the account's phone number. Deletion cannot be undone.

## 9. Security

- All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with **HTTPS**;
- Passwords are never stored in plain text, only as a one-way hash;
- Sign-in is confirmed with an SMS code;
- You can block users and report content;
- Staff can access data only as needed for their tasks.

No service can guarantee 100% security, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures. If we discover a data breach, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.
