Tashkent, edition of 18 November 2026

This Personal Data Protection and Processing Policy (the Policy) sets out how personal data of users of the "Student Club" mobile app is collected, stored, processed, used, transferred and protected.

Personal data is processed in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" and other legislation.

1. General provisions

1.1. The Operator respects Users' right to privacy and takes the legal, organisational and technical measures needed to protect personal data.

1.2. By registering in and using the App, the User confirms that they have read and agree to this Policy.

1.3. If the User does not agree to this Policy, they must stop using the App and delete their account.

1.4. This Policy applies only to data processed within the App. Third-party websites and services reached through links in the App are governed by their own policies.

2. Terms and definitions

2.1. Operator - the sole proprietor who owns the App and organises the processing of personal data (see section 3 of this Policy).

2.2. App - the "Student Club" mobile app, its server side, interfaces and other functionality.

2.3. User - a natural person who has registered in or otherwise uses the App.

2.4. Account - the User's account in the App.

2.5. Personal data - information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

2.6. User content - listings, posts, stories, messages, photos, videos, voice messages and other materials the User publishes or sends in the App.

2.7. Processing - any action performed on personal data: collection, recording, organisation, storage, correction, use, transfer, anonymisation, blocking, deletion and destruction.

3. Data controller

3.1. Personal data operator: Sole proprietor Qo'shshayev Qahhor G'afurovich (the Operator, we or us).

3.2. Contact email: quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com.

4. Data processed by the Operator

4.1. The Operator may process the following User data:

  • a) phone number - to create the account, sign in to the App and protect the account;
  • b) first name, last name and place of study (university) - to show the User to other students and connect them with fellow students;
  • c) university email - if the User chooses to provide it;
  • d) profile photo - if the User uploads one;
  • e) User content;
  • f) correspondence with the Operator and support, complaints and requests;
  • g) location data - only in the cases described in section 5 of this Policy;
  • h) technical data: User and device identifiers, push notification token, device model, operating system and App version, interface language, date and time of requests, IP address and technical error logs.

4.2. The Operator does not request or intentionally process special categories of personal data (health, religious or political beliefs, biometric data and the like).

4.3. The User agrees not to publish excessive personal data in the App, or personal data of third parties without their consent.

5. Device permissions

5.1. Location. The App asks for location access only when the User starts a related action (for example, to show the distance to a listing or discount, or to mark an address on the map). The App does not track location in the background.

5.2. Camera, microphone and photos. Access is requested only when the User starts taking a photo, recording a voice message, choosing a file or a similar action. The App uses only the files the User selects or records.

5.3. Notifications. Push notifications are sent only after the User allows them.

5.4. Granting permissions is voluntary. Without them, the rest of the App keeps working. The User can revoke permissions at any time in the device settings.

6. Purposes of processing

6.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • a) registering and identifying the User;
  • b) providing access to the App;
  • c) showing profiles, listings, posts and stories and connecting the User with fellow students;
  • d) delivering messages and notifications;
  • e) showing the distance to listings and discounts (if the User allows it);
  • f) handling requests and complaints;
  • g) keeping the App secure and preventing fraud, spam and abuse;
  • h) detecting and fixing errors in the App;
  • i) complying with the law and requests from competent authorities;
  • j) protecting the rights and legitimate interests of the Operator, Users and third parties.

6.2. The Operator does not sell Users' personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

7. Legal grounds for processing

7.1. The Operator processes personal data on the following grounds:

  • a) the User's consent;
  • b) the need to provide the App's features to the User;
  • c) compliance with the law;
  • d) protection of the rights and legitimate interests of the Operator, Users and third parties.

8. What other users can see

8.1. The User's name, profile photo and university, as well as the listings, posts and stories they publish, are visible to other Users of the App.

8.2. The User decides in settings who can see their phone number and last-seen time, and who can message them.

8.3. Private messages are visible only to the participants of the conversation, and group messages only to the group's members.

9. Transfer of data to third parties

9.1. The Operator transfers personal data only to the extent needed to run the App, to the following parties:

  • a) Eskiz (Uzbekistan) - to send SMS verification codes: phone number and SMS text;
  • b) Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) - to deliver push notifications: device token and notification text;
  • c) Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) - the hosting provider whose servers run the App;
  • d) competent state authorities - only in the cases and manner provided by law.

9.2. Data is transferred to anyone else only with the User's separate consent or on another lawful basis.

10. Where data is stored

10.1. Personal data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany (Nuremberg).

10.2. By using the App, the User consents to their personal data being stored and processed outside the Republic of Uzbekistan.

11. Retention periods

11.1. Personal data is kept for the following periods:

  • a) stories - removed from the feed 24 hours after publication and kept in the User's private archive; their media files are deleted after 365 days;
  • b) account data and other User content - until the User deletes it or their account;
  • c) technical logs - for as long as needed for the purpose they were collected for.

11.2. When the account is deleted, the User's personal data is deleted from the Operator's servers, except data that must be kept by law.

12. Data protection

12.1. To protect personal data against unlawful access, alteration, disclosure and destruction, the Operator takes the following measures:

  • a) all communication between the App and the server is encrypted over HTTPS;
  • b) passwords are never stored in plain text, only as a one-way hash;
  • c) the phone number is confirmed with an SMS code at registration and password reset;
  • d) Users can block other Users and report content;
  • e) access to data is granted only within job duties and to the extent needed.

12.2. The User is responsible for keeping their password, phone and other means of account access confidential. The Operator is not liable for the consequences of the User sharing their credentials with third parties.

12.3. If a data breach is detected, the Operator notifies the User and the competent authority as required by law.

13. User rights

13.1. The User has the right to:

  • a) receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • b) request correction of incomplete, outdated or inaccurate data;
  • c) delete listings, posts, stories or the whole account;
  • d) withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • e) lodge a complaint with the competent state authority;
  • f) exercise other rights provided by law.

13.2. To exercise these rights, the User sends a request to the Operator at the email in section 17 of this Policy. The Operator replies within 10 working days.

13.3. Deleting data or withdrawing consent may make some App features unavailable or lead to the deletion of the account.

14. Account deletion

14.1. The User can delete their account at any time in the App's Settings.

14.2. If the User cannot sign in, they can email quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the account's phone number.

14.3. Account deletion cannot be undone.

15. Minors

15.1. The App is intended for university students and is not directed at children.

15.2. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are strictly prohibited. Details are on the "Child Safety Standards" page: legal.studentclub.uz/child-safety.html.

16. Changes to the Policy

16.1. The Operator may amend this Policy unilaterally.

16.2. Users are notified of material changes in advance through the App. The current edition is identified by the date and version at the top of this page.

16.3. Continuing to use the App after changes take effect means the User accepts the new edition.

17. Operator contact details

17.1. For questions and requests about the processing of personal data: quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com.
